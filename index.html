Meet your teacher Hello!

My name is Delia Knapp. I am a self-taught artist, and I have been drawing since I was a little girl. I am fluent in Spanish, and I homeschool my five-year-old daughter.

Art brings my soul peace, and I believe all children have an inner artist.

Learn with us through our unique group lessons! We focus on art, Spanish, sensory play, and crafts. Classes are bilingual, taught in both English and Spanish.

Nuestra filosofía

Every child has an inner artist. We slow down, get our hands messy, and let curiosity lead, one color and one new word at a time.
What we do

Paint, play, and learn in two languages

Each class blends three things kids love, so they learn a new language without even noticing.

Art group lessonsClases de arte en grupo

Little hands learn big skills: shapes, shading, and color mixing. We study animals, fruit, and flowers, and every child leaves with art to hang on the fridge.

Bilingual learningAprendizaje bilingüe

English and Spanish side by side. Kids learn colors, numbers, animals, and everyday phrases in both languages while they create, count, and chat about their work.

Crafts & sensory playManualidades y juego sensorial

Bug gardens, honeycomb trays, rainbow clouds, and water-bead bins. Themed, hands-on play that builds fine motor skills and plenty of giggles.
